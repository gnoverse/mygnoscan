mygnoscan / chain analytics vision
POC · synthetic data

This is a vision prototype for turning the data flowing through tx-indexer into a picture of the whole chain — how it grows, where value moves, who builds, and what it costs to store state. Every number here is synthetic but shaped to be realistic (growth curves, weekly seasonality, power-law distributions). The point is the charts and the questions they answer, not the values. Each card explains why it matters and is tagged by what it would take to ship:

✅ LIVE already in the indexer — just an aggregation in mygnoscan 🟡 INDEX needs GnoEvent replay / new bucketing 🔴 RPC needs chain state (balances, voting power, tallies)
